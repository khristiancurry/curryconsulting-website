The idea is simple: deliver practical, scalable solutions that give organizations the structure and efficiency they need to grow with confidence.

Meet the Founder

A Virginian native, Khristian Curry has always been driven by a passion for innovation and problem-solving. After earning his technical degree from Clemson University, he built a career developing automation and digital solutions that make complex systems and processes simple.

With experience across modern automation tools, database systems, and digitalization platforms, Khristian brings a versatile skill set to every project — blending technical depth with strategic thinking to create custom solutions that are both effective and scalable.

At Curry Consulting Services, his mission is simple: empower businesses with innovative applications, efficient automation, and robust database strategies that enhance growth and reduce waste.
